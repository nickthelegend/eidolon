# Eidolon art direction
- **Style:** grainy painterly editorial illustration, cinematic night storm, visible brush texture and film grain, soft volumetric light, dramatic but calm composition.
- **Palette:** storm navy #141a26, near-black #0b0c0e, ember orange #ff7a3d (the trader), spectral pale green #d9f99d (the ghost), a touch of slate blue #2e3d63.
- **Subjects:** a lone trader lit in ember facing a translucent spectral double of themselves; a glowing horizontal price line between them; candle wicks like lightning; two coins or tokens, one solid ember, one ghostly; hourglass of 60 seconds.
- **Banned:** real brands or logos, Bitcoin "₿" glyph, readable text or numbers, real people, other designers' artwork.

## v2: studio obsidian and glass (Nov 9)

Replaces the painterly storm set for the near-black trading UI (Sora / Inter / JetBrains Mono, background #050505). Generated with ChatGPT's image model in one chat, one image per message. Source PNGs: `art-src/v2/`; web copies: `packages/web/public/art/v2/` (WebP q85; avatars 256px, objects 512px, wide images 1536px wide). Contact sheet: `art-src/v2/contact-sheet.jpg`.

- **Style line (end of every prompt):** "Premium 3D product render, studio lighting, glossy obsidian black glass and brushed dark metal, one warm ember-orange (#ff7a3d) rim light, subtle pale lime (#d9f99d) glow only on ghost objects which are made of frosted translucent glass, pure near-black #050505 background, minimal, high-end, crisp, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no Bitcoin symbol."
- **Transparent assets add:** "Transparent PNG background with a real alpha channel, no ground shadow, centered, 1024x1024."
- **Motif:** every object has a solid obsidian version (you) and a frosted-glass ghost twin (your ghost). Eidolon is Greek for phantom, so masks are classical Greek theatre masks with laurel, abstract, never people.
- **Palette:** #050505 background, ember #ff7a3d (you), pale lime #d9f99d (ghost only), obsidian black, brushed dark metal.
- **Banned:** text, letters, numbers, logos, the Bitcoin glyph, real people, other designers' work.
