# Eidolon art direction
- **Style:** grainy painterly editorial illustration, cinematic night storm, visible brush texture and film grain, soft volumetric light, dramatic but calm composition.
- **Palette:** storm navy #141a26, near-black #0b0c0e, ember orange #ff7a3d (the trader), spectral pale green #d9f99d (the ghost), a touch of slate blue #2e3d63.
- **Subjects:** a lone trader lit in ember facing a translucent spectral double of themselves; a glowing horizontal price line between them; candle wicks like lightning; two coins or tokens, one solid ember, one ghostly; hourglass of 60 seconds.
- **Banned:** real brands or logos, Bitcoin "₿" glyph, readable text or numbers, real people, other designers' artwork.
